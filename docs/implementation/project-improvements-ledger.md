## What is established

- **Builds and tests.** Full Fabric, Paper and NeoForge builds with `:common:test`, Tier 1, Tier 2 (and Tier 3 where the line has it), packaging and release checks passed on every line at 1.21.1 `0ae20c5f`, 1.21.10 `8a6457bd`, 1.21.11 `b8828f5a`, 26.1 `f8f31bb6`, 26.2 `6f708110`, including all 82 new preset/undo JUnit identities. No `*.java` file changed after those commits (`git diff <built>..HEAD -- '*.java'` is empty on every line), so the built jars are the shipped bytes of the branch heads.
- **Correctness fixes with regression coverage.** Diagnostic-export lifecycle retention, the reconnect receipt race, Folia loaded-terrain-versus-disk correction ordering (bounded, per-player, swept on removal), the header-freshness early return, the rig consumer refusal bug, and the loaded-correction retention after store/empty replies. The final review confirmed the per-player keying, sweeps, registration-bound batches, epoch-before-decode receipt rejection and the untouched tscache/store/header doctrine.
- **Performance.** V27 (26.2, four Fabric clients plus a Folia server, fixed heaps, 30 FPS pacing) passed under the median-plus-two-of-three rule; the third-pair server tick-delay p99 exceeded its individual bound and is recorded. Limits: the frame rows are pacing-saturated at the 30 FPS cap, tick-delay noise is about the size of the 10% budget, 32/4/1 is the fixed operating point both arms ran at (not a value compared against the defaults), and the six functional prerequisites ran on Fabric/Paper bytes that predate the last two correctness commits while Folia correctness holds on both measured arms.
- **Final independent reviews.** Reviews 1–3 found nothing actionable. Review 4's R04-1 (a wrong-loader jar could make a Fabric/Paper profile launch-ready) is fixed on all five lines with negative controls. Review 5's R05-1..5 (signal-stop escalation losing the runner's cleanup receipt, group signal by reaped PID, foreign-X-server display adoption, inherited launch environment, descriptive metric schema) are fixed on all five lines with tests (`tools/rig` 621/621 per line). Review 6's R06-3..6 are documented evidence limits; R06-1 (Folia correction re-sends identical bodies, unmeasured) and R06-2 (correction is own-region only) are recorded follow-ups, not regressions.
- **Tooling gates.** The CI-equivalent offline checks (compat, lines, rig, verify, tests, client-preset-tools suites; settings inventory/reference; test-move inventory; artifact exclusion; exact source-ref comparison) pass locally on every line at the final heads.
