# LSS 0.24.0 release preparation

Prepared 2026-11-27 on the four shipping lines: 26.3, 1.21.1, 26.2 and 1.21.11. **Tagged and
published the same day** (10:28 to 10:51 UTC), after the visual pass and the one fix it led to
(see "Visual pass" and "Published"). 26.1 and 1.21.10 are frozen
([decision 0001](../decisions/0001-line-tiers.md)). Identities, counts and jar hashes are in the
[manifest](../implementation/2026-11-27-v0.24.0-release-preparation.json). Notes:
`v0.24.0-release-notes/` (one file per line).

## Scope

The code of [0.24.0-beta.1](2026-11-23-v0.24.0-beta.1-release-preparation.md)
([plan](2026-11-21-v0.24.0-plan.md)) under the stable version, plus:

- **#436**, merged after the beta: a `/lss fillmap stop` or a Fill Map press that waits for the
  client's cache to load reaches the saved record, and a stop in a session that downloads
  nothing says that nothing is running.
- **The stable review's fold of #436** (plan §11): a press after such a stop is the one saved,
  and a stop is honoured in every dialect (older-dialect sessions load the cache too).
- **A gametest fix:** `diskReadServesDoNotSeedTheDirtyContentFilter` asks again on a fresh
  service when its one disk read ran out of time, inside a budget of 2400 ticks
  ([flake catalog](../operations/test-flakes.md)). It was the first 26.3 gate's one red.
- **A fix from the visual pass** (#442; ports #443, #444, #445): a map fill gets its share of a
  request batch also while the scan is busy. Before it, Fill Map delivered nothing on a world
  that was still being generated. Client only.
- **Notes and README** tightened after the review; no default changed.
- 26.2 and 1.21.11 receive the whole release as a port from main; 1.21.1 moves from the beta to
  the stable version.

So stable is not byte-for-byte the beta's code (decision 0002 says "same code"): the client-side
Fill Map record changed in about 30 lines, all of it pinned by tests through the real cache files.

| Row | Result |

| M3, M5 | blocked by M2 and M4; both passed with the client's LOD distance at 32 chunks |
| M1 map switched on after the download | failed as briefed: the brief expected no new tiles before the press (see below) |

**The finding.** Fill Map made no progress at the default settings. The fill added nothing to a
request while the scan asked for a single column nobody holds, and on a world that is still
being generated out to the LOD distance the scan always does (the client's own diagnostics during
the stalled fill: `Budget: used=253/253`). With the distance at 32 chunks the same fill finished
in nine seconds. The live checks before the pass had not seen it: their server has a
pregenerated world and generation off.

**The fix** (#442, plan §11 "The visual pass"): the fill gets what the scan leaves of a request
batch and never less than a quarter of it. Evidence, all on the final code:

| Check | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rig, development client, Xaero's World Map, fresh world, generation on, LOD 96 | 0 of 4,631 delivered 243 s after the press | 4,681 of 4,681 in 31 s, first after 7 s |
| Rig, three sessions, pregenerated world (the original check) | | 18 of 18 laws: 0 columns before the press, 6,304 owed columns sent once, 0 at the next join |
| Production stack on a private display (Sodium, Voxy, Xaero), default settings, fresh world | | scan batch full (`259/259`), 3,868 owed: first delivery 14 s after the typed `/lss fillmap`, 3,866 in 70 s, "Map fill finished"; generation went on (4,958 to 6,102 chunks) |
| The same with the stop row | | 16 of 16 laws: "already running", the stop held for 20 s with 2,804 still owed, second stop "No map fill is running", the restart finished them |

Four unit tests, eight deliberate breaks (all red), one independent read of the fix (no defect;
wording and two test gaps folded). The Fill Map rows were not played a second time in a visible
window: the two production-stack runs stand in for that, with the real jars and mods but a
software renderer and a typed command instead of the button (the button itself, its toast and
its answers passed in the first run). The brief for a visible repeat is
`~/projects/lss-0.24.0/ASTRA-RERUN-FILLMAP.md`. Scripts and evidence:
`~/servers/lss-fill-busy/` and `~/.local/state/lss-release-prep-v0.24.0/fill-busy/`.

**What stands as it is.** M1: the map gained tiles before the press because the server sent
those columns anyway (its copy was newer than the client's: 507 of 4,148, and the fill then
announced exactly the rest, 3,641); with Map Ingestion on they are written to the map like any
terrain. B1: a straight-edged patch of water was missing far out for some seconds after a long
flight and was whole on the next look; not reproduced. The #429 reporter describes something
similar with a lot of water in view; a new issue was invited.

## Final quick review

One reader over `v0.23.0..b34d5055a` and the notes, before the tags: no blocker. Every command,
button, settings key, JVM flag and number in the notes exists as written; no default changed
beyond the intended ones; no protocol change; no leftover debug output; every new language key
is in all three languages. Three notes for support, none a change: a volume that reports 0 free
bytes holds generation (by design); clients on older versions are not told when a low-disk hold
ends; with the hold switched off the store backfill keeps its own 2 GiB floor (the README says
so, the notes do not).
