## Production correctness assessment

- **Loaded-probe epochs remain valid.** `LoadedProbeGuard.capture/current` binds the processor instance, registration identity, non-retired state, dimension, position, and the same stripe token. Token rotation uses new objects, avoiding an integer wrap/ABA issue. `OffThreadProcessor.invalidateTimestamps` rotates tokens under the mailbox lock before appending the lossless invalidation. Both Paper paths capture before native serialization; the router validates both ordinary and paired probes. `LoadedProbeGuardTest`, `ProbeContainmentTest`, and `RegionProbeSchedulingTest.synchronousCapturePrecedesSerializerAndFreshCaptureRemainsUsable` explicitly exercise invalidation during serialization. Stripe collisions reject otherwise usable data conservatively; they cannot authorize another position's bytes.
- **Late corrections retain bounded per-player authority.** The map belongs to `AbstractPlayerRequestState`, not a global position table. Admission records the exact disk order before external submission and removes it on refusal. Completion/consumption checks retain the disk order and generation, and consumption checks the same data object. Retention is 512 attempts, 2,000,000 raw bytes, and 10 seconds from the original admission; newer frontiers do not renew the deadline. `takeLateProbe` and `consumeLateProbe` require older pending and enqueued work for the position to drain. Corrections therefore cannot overtake an older queued disk body. Invalidation, retirement, range loss, expiry, and replacement admission remove obsolete authority. These boundaries are directly pinned in `PairedIngressLifecycleTest` and the threaded `PaperPumpRouterAlignmentTest`.
- **Registration replacement is contained.** `runRegionProbe` rejects a retired/replaced state before native access and again within the result-map merge. `consumeRegionProbes` requires the current registration and dimension. Removal retires the registration, removes region/held results, discards handoff/late state, and passes the registration into reader/generation cleanup. Tests include `retiredCallbackCannotOverwriteSuccessorBatchWithSameUuid` and `retirementDuringSerializationCannotPublishIntoSuccessor`.
- **Reconnect receipts retain their owner.** `ClientColumnProcessor.drainColumnQueue` checks the epoch again after polling, reports a replacement receipt taken by an old drain, and checks epoch/active ownership again before dispatch. `teardownBetweenEpochCheckAndPollCannotAcceptANewSessionColumn` forces the exact check/poll interleaving and asserts no dispatch, a forgotten replacement stamp, one ingest failure, and zero queue debt. This is a substantive race test, not just a source-shape assertion.
- **Delivery/accounting boundaries are preserved.** Corrections use the ordinary enqueue/send path and mark completion after accepted enqueue; corrective send counters depend on successful actual send. Header/store/empty fallback retention is intentional and excludes ghost, stale, or rejected outcomes. The relevant coverage is in `CorrectiveSendAccountingTest`, `ReadFreshnessRungTest`, and `PaperPumpRouterAlignmentTest`.
